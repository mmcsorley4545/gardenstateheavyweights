Our History

The Garden State Heavyweight program was founded in 2019 by President Michael Howell and Vice Presidents Mahdi Pettis and Michael McSorley. Stationed out of Woodbridge New Jersey we have been home to strong high level athletes numbering to over 30 collegiate athletes.

Our program’s goal is to create hard-working, preserving, fundamentally sound boys and girls basketball players that have a nonnegotiable toughness. If this sounds like the family you want to be apart of you have found the right spot click on the forms to become a Heavyweight.
